                        Install on your phone

                        Wanderlog then opens from your home screen like an app, and works without a connection: your trip, bookings and confirmation numbers are saved on the phone.

                        1. iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
                        2. Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

                        Open the app once while online after each update so the latest version is saved for offline use. Map areas you've looked at are kept for offline viewing.

                          Forgot your passphrase?

                          Nobody can recover it, which is what keeps your trip private. If it's lost, you can remove the encrypted trip from this device and start fresh.

                          Encryption

                          Lock your trip with a passphrase. It's encrypted on your device before it's saved or uploaded, so Supabase, and anyone who got into it, only ever sees scrambled data.

                          • Your travel partner needs the same passphrase. Tell them in person.
                          • The family link keeps working, without confirmation numbers, booking links or notes. If you've already shared it, copy the new link from Share afterwards.
                          • If you forget the passphrase, your trip can't be recovered, by Supabase or anyone else. Save it in a password manager.

                          On Your trip is encrypted with your passphrase. Only devices where you've entered it can read it.
